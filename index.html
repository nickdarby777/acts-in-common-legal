1. What the App is for

Acts in Common helps members of a church community coordinate practical support (for example, requests, groups, messaging, and related notifications). Processing your information is necessary to provide those features.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Account and authentication data — such as email address and credentials used to sign in (handled through our authentication provider).
  • Profile and community data — such as name, contact details you choose to add, church membership, group membership, support requests, messages, reactions, and similar content you submit in the App.
  • Photos and media you choose to upload — for example a profile image or images attached to broadcasts, when you use those features and grant permission.
  • Camera — used only when you choose to scan a church invite QR code; images are processed for that purpose.
  • Push notification token — a device token so we can deliver notifications you opt into.
  • Approximate or precise location — only where the operating system or maps features require it (for example to show or confirm an address on a map). You can control location access in device settings.
  • Technical and security data — such as basic diagnostics needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, and maintain security (for example through our backend and infrastructure providers).

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App and its features;
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • Show relevant church and community content to you and, where appropriate, to other members of your church community according to the App’s rules and your actions (for example, requests you create or messages you send);
  • Send push notifications when you enable them;
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

If laws such as the UK or EU GDPR apply, we rely on appropriate bases including performance of a contract (providing the App you asked for), legitimate interests (for example securing the service and improving features, balanced against your rights), and consent where required (for example for certain optional permissions or marketing, if offered).

5. Sharing with service providers

We use trusted infrastructure providers to run the App, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, and related backend services.
  • Expo — build and delivery infrastructure; push notification delivery may involve Expo’s services when you enable notifications.
  • Google — when you use map or place-related features, limited information may be sent to Google’s services according to Google’s policies and your device settings.

These providers process data under agreements that require them to protect personal data and use it only to provide services to us. We do not sell your personal information.
